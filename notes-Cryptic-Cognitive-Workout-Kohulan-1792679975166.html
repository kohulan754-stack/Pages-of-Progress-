​I always thought cryptic crosswords made zero sense, but turns out it’s basically just a code to break. The first rule is to ignore what the sentence actually sounds like—the clue is deliberately trying to trick you. Every clue is split into two main parts: a standard definition at one end, and a mini puzzle in the rest of it. You just have to spot the trigger words that tell you what to do, like swapping letters around, finding a hidden word, or dropping a letter. Once you figure out where the clue splits and what the instructions are, it’s super satisfying to solve.
