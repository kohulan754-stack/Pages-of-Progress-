Introduction to the Trolley Problem: A classic ethical dilemma from 1967 where you must decide whether to flip a switch to kill 1 worker to save 5.

Utilitarian View (Switch Scenario): About 90% of people say it's okay to flip the switch because maximizing well-being for the greatest number is seen as the right choice.

The Bridge Variation: Changing the scenario so you have to physically push a large man off a bridge to stop the trolley.

Deontological / Emotional Reaction: Only about 10% agree with pushing the man, showing our instinctual aversion to deliberately causing someone's death compared to collateral damage.
