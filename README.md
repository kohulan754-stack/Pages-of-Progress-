Daily Quest

A Record of Learning and Progress

I am a first-year B.E. Computer Science and Engineering student at SRM Valliammai Engineering College. This repository is a record of the daily quests assigned as part of my English course.

These quests go beyond regular classroom learning. They involve reading, writing, speaking, listening, and exploring different areas of knowledge. Each activity gives me an opportunity to learn something new, improve the way I communicate, and understand ideas from different perspectives.

Purpose

My aim is not just to complete the assigned tasks, but to learn something meaningful from each one.

Through these activities, I want to improve my ability to express my thoughts clearly, understand what others communicate, develop my vocabulary, and become more confident in presenting my ideas. I also want to explore subjects beyond my academic syllabus and build a habit of continuous learning.

As a CSE student, I believe that technical knowledge alone is not enough. Communication, critical thinking, and the ability to understand people and ideas are equally important in both professional and personal life.

Areas of Learning

- Reading: Understanding texts, exploring ideas, and developing vocabulary.
- Writing: Expressing thoughts clearly and organising ideas effectively.
- Speaking: Improving confidence, fluency, and the ability to communicate with others.
- Listening: Paying attention to different perspectives and understanding information accurately.
- General Knowledge: Learning about topics beyond the classroom and broadening my understanding of the world.
- Reflection: Looking back at what I have learned and identifying how I can apply it in real life.

Quest Reports

Each quest has its own report containing the assigned work, important takeaways, and personal reflections wherever relevant.

The reports are organised individually so that my work remains easy to access and my progress can be followed over time.

My Approach

I see these quests as an opportunity to develop habits that go beyond completing assignments. Every activity may not teach me something immediately useful, but each one can contribute to the way I think, learn, and communicate.

I want to approach these tasks with curiosity, complete them sincerely, and take something valuable from the experience. Over time, this repository will serve as a record of not just the work I have completed, but also the progress I have made.

Ongoing Learning

This repository will be updated regularly as new quests are assigned and completed. It represents my ongoing effort to improve through practice, reflection, and consistent learning.
